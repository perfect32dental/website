Dental emergencies

Severe pain, facial swelling, bleeding that won't stop, a broken tooth, or a tooth knocked clean out. Don't wait for an appointment slot — ring us.

  • Knocked-out adult tooth: hold it by the crown, never the root. Put it back in the socket if you can, or keep it in milk, and get to us within the hour.
  • Facial swelling with fever or trouble swallowing: urgent. Call immediately.
  • Toothache: painkillers you'd normally take and a cold compress. Never put aspirin directly on the gum — it burns the tissue.
