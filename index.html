Why people travel to us

The scan, the plan and the treatment all happen here

Most clinics send you elsewhere for a CBCT and book you back a week later. We don't.

  • In-house OPG and CBCT. Full 3D imaging on site, so implant planning happens the same day.
  • 3D intra-oral scanner. Digital impressions — no gagging on trays of putty.
  • Implant systems you can look up: Straumann, Neodent and Dentsply Ankylos — not unbranded fixtures.
  • Sterilisation you can ask about: N-class and B-class autoclaves, UV chambers, ultrasonic cleaner and glass bead sterilisers. Instruments are pouched and sealed individually.
  • CAD/CAM crowns and veneers in 72 hours, milled from a digital scan.
  • Open seven days, with evening sessions Monday to Saturday for people who work.
