Why people travel to us

The scan, the plan and the treatment all happen here

Most clinics send you elsewhere for a CBCT and book you back a week later. We don't.

  • In-house OPG and CBCT. Full 3D imaging on site, so implant planning happens the same day.
  • 3D intra-oral scanner. Digital impressions — no gagging on trays of putty.
  • Implant systems you can look up: Straumann, Neodent and Dentsply Ankylos — not unbranded fixtures.
  • Sterilisation you can ask about: N-class and B-class autoclaves, UV chambers, ultrasonic cleaner and glass bead sterilisers. Instruments are pouched and sealed individually.
  • CAD/CAM crowns and veneers in 72 hours, milled from a digital scan.
  • Open seven days, with evening sessions Monday to Saturday for people who work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from our patients in Ambala and surrounding areas.

Is dental implant treatment painful?

Not at all. Implants are placed under local anaesthesia with computer-guided CBCT 3D planning. Most patients report feeling little to no discomfort during or after the procedure, returning to normal activities within 24 hours.

Do you have in-house CBCT and OPG 3D scanning?

Yes, we have an in-house digital OPG, CBCT, and 3D intra-oral scanner. You do not need to travel across Ambala for diagnostic scans; everything is completed on-site during your initial visit.

Can a root canal treatment (RCT) be completed in a single sitting?

Yes, in most indicated cases, root canal treatment is performed painlessly in a single visit using advanced rotary endodontics and digital apex locators.

What are your clinic opening hours and are you open on Sundays?

We are open 7 days a week! Monday to Saturday: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM. Sunday: 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM.
