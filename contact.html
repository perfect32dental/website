Questions

Things people ask us

Does a root canal hurt?

Not the way its reputation suggests. The tooth is fully numbed first, and most patients tell us afterwards it felt like having a large filling done. The pain people remember is usually the infection before treatment, not the treatment itself.

How long does an implant take?

The placement itself is usually under an hour. The bone then needs time to fuse to the implant — typically three to six months — before the final crown goes on. We'll scan you on our CBCT first and give you an exact timeline for your case.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

You're welcome to walk in and we'll fit you in if we can, but booking means you won't wait. If you're in pain, call ahead — we'll tell you the quickest time to come.

How much will it cost?

An examination is the only way to quote accurately. After it, you get a written plan with the cost of each item before anything begins. Indicative ranges are on the treatments page.

I'm terrified of the dentist. Can you help?

Yes, and you're far from unusual. Tell us when you book. We'll give you a longer, quieter slot, explain everything before we do it, and stop the moment you raise a hand.

Do you have evening appointments?

Yes — Monday to Saturday we run a second session from 5pm to 8pm, which suits most people who work. Sunday is mornings only, 11am to 2pm.

How do I change or cancel my appointment?

Message us on WhatsApp with your reference number, or call. A few hours' notice lets us give the slot to someone who needs it.
