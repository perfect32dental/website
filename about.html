About the clinic

A family practice in Model Town, Ambala — opposite Ambala Club — treating the same families for over 24 years.

Our approach

Slow down, explain, then treat

Most people who walk in have put the visit off, sometimes for years, usually because they expect it to hurt. So the first thing we do is look properly — with imaging taken here — and tell you the truth about what we find, including when the answer is "leave it alone and keep an eye on it".

Then you get a written plan with costs. You decide what to do and when. If money is the constraint, say so — there is almost always a staged approach that deals with the urgent thing first.

  • Instruments autoclaved between every patient
  • In-house OPG and CBCT — full 3D imaging without leaving the building
  • 3D intra-oral scanning instead of putty impression trays
  • Referral onwards if something is outside our scope

Sterilisation, in detail

Because people are right to ask. Instruments go through N-class and B-class autoclaves, UV chambers, an ultrasonic cleaner and glass bead sterilisers. Everything is packed and sealed in individual pouches, opened in front of you, to prevent cross-infection.

What we have on site

In-house OPG and CBCT, a 3D intra-oral scanner, digital intra-oral camera, RVG, fibre-optic ultrasonic scalers, a soft-tissue diode laser, a piezosurgery unit, laser whitening, a fibre-optic implant motor and rotary endodontics. CAD/CAM crowns and veneers are milled in 72 hours.

Your dentists

Who you'll see

Both dentists are registered with the Haryana State Dental Council. Registration numbers are listed so you can verify them.

Trained in implantology with the DGOI in Germany. Formerly Lecturer at BRS Dental College, Panchkula, and Dental Surgeon at Civil Hospital, Ambala. He places the clinic's implants and plans the full-mouth rehabilitation cases.

Dr. Shallu Batra

BDS · Certified Specialist in Smile Designing

Leads the clinic's cosmetic work — whitening, veneers and complete digital smile makeovers. Her approach is to plan a smile around the patient's own face and age, not to a template.
